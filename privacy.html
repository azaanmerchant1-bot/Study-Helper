Privacy & Rules

StudyAI is a student project. It is not an official school website.

What we store on your device

Your browser may save:

  • the last study set you generated
  • sets you save
  • your daily free-use count
  • your streak
  • Match best time
  • flagged questions and feedback

This stays on your computer unless you clear site data.

What you should not paste

  • passwords
  • full name, address, or phone number
  • other people’s private information
  • anything you would not show a teacher

Only paste study notes you are allowed to use.

How the AI works

When you click Generate, your notes are sent to the StudyAI server so it can make quiz questions. The AI can make mistakes. Use Flag question or Explain this if something looks wrong.

Feedback

If you send feedback or flag a question, that text may be saved so the site can be improved. Do not include private information in feedback.

Rules

  • Use StudyAI for studying.
  • Be kind in feedback.
  • Do not try to break the site or skip the daily limit.
  • Free use is limited each day.
